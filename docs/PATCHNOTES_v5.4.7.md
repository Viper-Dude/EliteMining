**Release Date:** 2026-October-24

## New Features & Improvements

### Mining Reports

- **New:** A search box finds reports by date, system, ring, ship, session type, mineral, material or comment.
- **New:** The report list shows the newest 100 sessions, with a Load more button for the rest.
- **New:** The Time filter has Last 6 months and This year.
- **Changed:** The totals at the bottom now always cover every matching report, not only the ones loaded.

### Hotspots Finder

- **New:** An Auto-Save option for Spansh and Both searches saves new hotspots to your local database automatically, without changing anything already saved.
- **Changed:** The PowerPlay option now shows as unticked while it is greyed out.

### Voiceattack Profile updated to v5.4.7

- **Fixed:** When Thrust Up was not selected in the VA controlls tab, the firegroup change could switch to the wrong firegroup.
