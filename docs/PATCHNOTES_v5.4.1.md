**Release Date:** 2026-September-25

## New Features & Improvements

### Hotspots Finder

- **Rank Minerals by Sell Price** — right-click a ring or system to see hotspot minerals ranked by best sell price, with distance, station, station type, and PowerPlay info. Right-click a row to copy the system name or open it in Inara, EDSM, or Spansh.
- **Rank All Minerals in System** — checks every hotspot mineral known anywhere in the system, not just one ring.
- **"+ Also Check"** — include non-hotspot minerals like Osmium, Palladium, Gold, and Silver in the ranking.
- **Auto Prices** — toggle. Jump into a system with known hotspots and it automatically checks live prices and shows the ranking, no click needed. Off by default.
- **Find Best Mining System** — pick a system you want to sell at, and find the best nearby mining system and mineral to bring there, ranked by price. Includes a Mining Type filter (Any / Core / Laser) and a PowerPlay mode for local-only prices.
- **PowerPlay Mode** — restrict prices to stations in the current system, for undermining/reinforcing where you can only sell locally.
