What's new in EliteMining v5.6.9

New Features & Improvements
- General code optimalization.
- Commodity Market searches are faster and find more stations with fresh prices, both nearby and galaxy-wide.
- Low Temperature Diamonds is now named LTD consistently across the app.
- Commodity Market results now show System and Station in separate columns, with full station names.
- The Distance Calculator now uses colours that match your theme, with a larger distance result.
- Hotspots Finder can now find systems or rings that have two chosen minerals, such as Platinum and Painite.

Bug Fixes
- Commodity Market no longer mistakes newly built colony settlements for fleet carriers, so they no longer appear in Fleet Carrier searches or vanish when Exclude Carriers is ticked.
- Ocellus stations and megaships now show the correct station type and appear in the right filters.
- The Distance Calculator no longer stays stuck on "Calculating..." when you edit a system mid-calculation.
- Picking a Home System from the suggestion list now saves the full system name.
- The Distance Calculator no longer stalls for up to a minute when the online system lookup is slow.
