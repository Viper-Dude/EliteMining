What's new in EliteMining v5.6.3

Hotfixes in v5.6.3

- Fixed Sell My Hold freezing and going blank while a mining session was running.

- Fixed unwanted column stretching in Sell My Hold.

- Fixed excessive decimal places in the Sell My Hold LS column.

Included changes from v5.6.2

- Reworked Mining Statistics with its own tab, filters, mineral performance, recent progress and your best and latest mining locations.

- Added Iridium to the Metals list under Commodity Market → Trade Commodities.

- Fixed the Customize Columns scrollbar to match the selected theme throughout the app.

- Added Sell My Hold under Commodity Market to compare cargo sale options, show station types and reserve mission cargo.

- Added a Sidebar menu option to swap Cargo Monitor.
