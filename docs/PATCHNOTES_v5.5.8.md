**Release Date** · November 10, 2026

## New Features & Improvements

- Press Escape to clear the selection in any table.
- Fixed minor bugs in the Modern Theme.
- In Reports, the Mining Card button is shorter, and the buttons and summary text are slightly smaller to prevent clipping.
- Updated the voice callout to “Retracting scoop. Setting power and fire group.” in all sound packs.
- **Fixed** the “Starting Mining Sequence” callout in the US 01 Male sound pack so it plays the correct audio.

### VoiceAttack Profile Update (v5.5.8)

- Added new voice phrases to enable, disable, or check the status of 15 EliteMining settings. For example, say “Enable cargo scoop,” “Toggle off cargo scoop retraction during a reset mining sequence,” or “What is the cargo scoop status?”
- Spoken feedback and VoiceAttack log messages now consistently use “enabled” and “disabled.” Existing phrases still work.
- General profile optimizations.

### Important Notes

- Check all key and joystick bindings after every profile update.
