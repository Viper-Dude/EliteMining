**Release Date:** 2026-November-26

## New Features & Improvements

- **Mining Controls tab: - Power settings** Now support "Choose Balanced, Max Engines, or Max Shields" for your ship's power after laser mining.

## Bug Fixes

- Buttons in the Modern themes no longer switch between two colours after hovering.

### VoiceAttack Profile - update to v5.7.5

- Toggle and power voice commands are simpler: say "Enable" or "Disable" to change a setting, "Say settings for" to hear it, and choose Balanced, Max Engines, or Max Shields for your power after laser mining.
- The "Import settings" voice command is removed, because settings already sync automatically.

### Important Notes 

- Remember to always check all your key and joystick bindings after every profile update.
