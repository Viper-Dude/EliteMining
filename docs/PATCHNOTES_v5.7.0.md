# <img src="https://github.com/Viper-Dude/EliteMining/blob/main/app/Images/logo_multi.ico" width="32" height="32"> EliteMining
**Release Date:** 2026-November-23

## New Features & Improvements

- The Hotspots Finder search results now have five views to switch between: Hotspots, Best Prices, Where to Mine, Acquisition and Merit Miner, so prices, best mining systems and acquisition systems no longer open in popups.
- Where to Mine lists the nearest mining systems with the best price and demand within your chosen selling distance.
- Auto Prices switches to Best Prices after each jump.
- New Merit Miner finds the nearest systems of your power where you can mine a chosen mineral for merits, and where to sell it.
- Right-click a Merit Miner result to see every buyer near that system, and the Acquisition view now says when a system belongs to another power.
- My Pledged Power is now set automatically from the game each time you log in.


## Bug Fixes

- Find Acquisition Systems now shows "Unoccupied" instead of a placeholder name in the Contesting Power column.
