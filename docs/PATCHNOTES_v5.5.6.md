# <img src="https://github.com/Viper-Dude/EliteMining/blob/main/app/Images/logo_multi.ico" width="32" height="32"> EliteMining
**Release Date:** 2026-November-07

## New Features & Improvements

### New Modern Theme
- New "Modern" theme with a cleaner look, rounded buttons and frames, and a compact Hotspots Finder search.
- Secondary Hotspots Finder filters now live under a collapsible "More filters" button that shows how many are active.
- Cargo Monitor now sits above Ship Presets and shows a fill bar.
- EDDN and Spansh status now show once in the bottom bar.
- The Theme button have now a selection for Elite Orange, Dark Gray and Modern.

### Commodity Market - Multiple Prices
- New "Multiple Prices" tab finds the best stations to sell up to 5 minerals at once.
- Stations buying the most of your minerals are listed first.

### Ring Finder - Find Sell Station
- Find Sell Station now works with "All Minerals" - pick which minerals to sell.

### Ring Finder - Best Minerals to Mine
- "Rank Minerals" is now a single right-click item called "Best Minerals to Mine".
- You can switch between "This Ring" and "Whole System" inside the window, without closing it.
- A new Refresh button updates the prices without closing the window.
- The window now shows which system or ring the prices are for.
- "+ Also Check" is now "+ Add Minerals".

### Voiceattack Profile update to v5.5.6
- General optimisations.


### Important Notes 

- Remember to always check all your key and joystick bindings after every profile update.
