============================================================
  EliteMining v5.7.5 — VoiceAttack Profile Update 
============================================================

This release includes an updated VoiceAttack profile (v5.7.5).

What's changed:
- Toggle and power voice commands are simpler: say "Enable" or "Disable" to change a setting, "Say settings for" to hear it, and choose Balanced, Max Engines, or Max Shields for your power after laser mining.
- The "Import settings" voice command is removed, because settings already sync automatically.

Keep the VoiceAttack profile option ticked on the next page to install the updated
profile. Follow the app's profile update instructions to import it into VoiceAttack.
After updating, please check all your key and joystick bindings.

------------------------------------------------------------
If you do NOT want to update the profile now, untick the option on the next
page. You can always re-run the installer later to update it.
------------------------------------------------------------
